Meet Our Visionary Leaders

Meet the visionary leaders driving YAD’s mission to empower Cambodian youth.

Discover Our Team

Governance

Mr. Chean Toing Ain

FOUNDER

  • Role: Visionary Leader
  • From: Phnom Penh
  • Education: PhD in Social Development
  • Awards: Global Humanitarian Award 2020

Dr. Sovannara Kim, the esteemed Founder of the Youth Advancement for Development Organization (YAD), stands as a beacon of hope for underprivileged youth across Cambodia. With a PhD in Social Development from the University of Cambridge, Dr. Kim has dedicated over two decades to empowering young minds through education and innovation. In 2015, she founded YAD with a vision to break the cycle of poverty by providing safe housing, digital literacy, and educational opportunities to rural students.

Her leadership has transformed the lives of thousands, earning her the prestigious Global Humanitarian Award in 2020. Dr. Kim’s unwavering commitment to social justice and youth empowerment has made YAD a cornerstone of community development in Cambodia, inspiring a generation to dream bigger and achieve greatness.
