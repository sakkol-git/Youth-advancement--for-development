Our Principle

Our Story

Emerging from the shadows of Cambodia’s post-Khmer Rouge era, the Youth Advancement for Development Organization (YAD) was founded to combat the deep rooted educational disparities that stifled a generation’s potential. With an unwavering commitment to equity, YAD empowers underprivileged youth by forging transformative pathways to higher education, digital literacy, and community development. We nurture resilience in young Cambodians, equipping them with the tools to rise above systemic challenges and become visionary leaders. Through safe housing, innovative training, and mentorship, YAD has ignited hope in thousands, enabling them to rewrite their futures and uplift their communities. Our mission transcends education it’s a movement to cultivate change makers who inspire progress and heal a nation. Since 2015, YAD has been a beacon of opportunity, illuminating the dreams of Cambodia’s youth and building a legacy of empowerment that echoes across generations, fostering a brighter, more inclusive tomorrow.

Our Vision

" TO TRANSFORM AND EMPOWER YOUTH TO BECOME FUTURE LEADERS WHO DRIVE POSITIVE CHANGE IN SOCIETY "

Our Mission

We're committed to

1. Expanding Educational Access & Innovation

  • Facilitate broad access to education for Cambodian youth through technological innovation and digital solutions
  • Remove educational barriers through targeted support programs and resources
  • Provide university education opportunities through scholarships, dormitory residential accommodation, and comprehensive youth development training

2. Building Community Impact & Leadership

  • Support disadvantaged communities through holistic educational programs encompassing English language skills, digital literacy, health awareness, and essential life skills
  • Implement sustainable financial support mechanisms for communities in need
  • Foster leadership development through our unique service-learning model, where dormitory residents serve as teaching assistants in poor local communities
  • Enable youth to develop practical leadership skills while gaining firsthand understanding of societal challenges

Our Core Value

Our core values promote action-oriented growth and leadership among youth and act as a directing compass for our team members to act accordingly in their daily conducts as they interact with themselves, peers, stakeholders, partners, and the communities:

ACTIONS:

  • Aspiration: Empowering youth to aim high and pursue meaningful goals.
  • Collaboration: Promoting teamwork, idea-sharing, and mutual support.
  • Thoughtfulness: Acting with empathy, care, and respect for others.
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty, ethics, and personal responsibility.
  • Opportunity: Expanding access to learning and growth experiences.
  • Nurturing: Guiding and encouraging youth to become confident leaders.
  • Social Responsibility: Motivating youth to address and impact societal issues.

Our Goal and Objective

Our strategic goal is to empower Cambodia's youth through comprehensive educational, leadership, and entrepreneurial development initiatives. By removing educational barriers, strengthening sustainable capacity building, and creating targeted opportunities, we aim to cultivate well-rounded leaders who can drive positive change within their communities and contribute to global development.

  • Objective 1: Dormitory and Youth Training Program (DYTP)
  • Objective 2: Empowering Youth through Digital Innovation & IT Literacy/Skills
  • Objective 3: Slum Community Education Initiative

These interconnected programs form the foundation of our holistic approach to youth development and educational access. Each initiative has been strategically designed to complement and reinforce the others, creating a comprehensive ecosystem of support that addresses both immediate educational needs and long-term development goals. Through this integrated approach, we aim to break the cycle of educational inequality while fostering sustainable pathways to personal and professional growth.
