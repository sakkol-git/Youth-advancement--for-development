Empowering Communities Through Events

Porridge for Hope

The "Porridge for Hope" event is a heartfelt initiative by YAD to nourish both the body and spirit of children in Phnom Penh’s slum communities. Held biannually, this event brings together volunteers, university students from our Dormitory and Youth Training Program (DYTP), and local families to distribute freshly prepared porridge, educational materials, and essential food supplies to over 250 children. The event is more than a meal distribution; it’s a celebration of community, hope, and resilience.

In our most recent event, held in Toulkok, we served nutritious porridge made with local ingredients, ensuring each child received a warm, healthy meal. Alongside the food, we provided notebooks, pens, and hygiene kits to support their education and well-being. The atmosphere was filled with laughter and music, as our team organized games and storytelling sessions, creating a joyful environment where children felt valued and cared for.

For many of these children, daily meals are not guaranteed, and access to school supplies is a luxury. "Porridge for Hope" addresses these immediate needs while fostering a sense of belonging. Our university student volunteers, who often come from similar backgrounds, shared their stories of overcoming challenges, inspiring the children to dream big. Parents also joined in, participating in workshops on nutrition and education, strengthening family support systems.

The impact of this event extends beyond the day itself. Many children reported feeling more motivated to attend school, and parents expressed gratitude for the community support. By partnering with local businesses and NGOs like the Taiwan Fund for Children and Families (TFCF), we ensure the event’s sustainability, allowing us to reach more children each year. "Porridge for Hope" is a testament to YAD’s belief that small acts of kindness can spark lasting change.

Foods and Materials Donation

Our "Foods and Materials Donation" event is a cornerstone of YAD’s mission to support the education and well-being of slum community children. Conducted quarterly in Phnom Penh’s most underserved areas, this initiative provides over 250 children with essential food items, school supplies, and learning materials. Each event is a vibrant gathering that combines practical support with community engagement, fostering hope and opportunity for young learners.

During our latest event in Kandal Province, we distributed rice, canned goods, and cooking oil to families, ensuring they had sustenance for weeks. For the children, we provided backpacks filled with textbooks, stationery, and art supplies, enabling them to engage fully in their studies. Our team also set up interactive learning stations where children practiced reading and math, guided by DYTP student mentors who brought enthusiasm and relatability to the activities.

The event’s impact is profound. For instance, Srey, a 12-year-old participant, shared how her new backpack and books made her feel “ready to learn and dream of becoming a teacher.” Parents attended sessions on budgeting and child health, gaining tools to support their children’s growth. The event also featured cultural performances, with children showcasing traditional Cambodian dances, boosting their confidence and sense of identity.

Through partnerships with corporate sponsors and local NGOs, we secure the resources needed to sustain this initiative. The involvement of our university student volunteers not only amplifies the event’s reach but also provides them with leadership experience, aligning with YAD’s dual-impact model. By addressing both immediate needs and long-term aspirations, "Foods and Materials Donation" empowers children to pursue education with dignity and hope.

School Supplies Donation

The "School Supplies Donation" drive is a transformative effort to equip slum students in Phnom Penh with the tools they need to succeed academically. Held annually, this event targets children who lack basic educational resources, such as notebooks, pens, backpacks, and uniforms. By providing these essentials, we aim to remove barriers to education and inspire a love for learning among over 300 young students.

Our most recent drive, held in a bustling slum community near Toulkok, saw volunteers and DYTP students distribute carefully curated school kits to children aged 6 to 15. Each kit included high-quality supplies, a durable backpack, and a hygiene pack with soap and toothbrushes. We also organized a mini “learning festival,” where children participated in storytelling, art workshops, and science experiments, sparking their curiosity and creativity.

The event’s impact was evident in the children’s smiles and enthusiasm. For example, 10-year-old Rith, who received his first-ever backpack, proudly said, “Now I can carry my books to school and study hard to become a doctor.” Teachers from local schools reported increased attendance following the event, as children felt more confident and prepared. Parents joined workshops on supporting their children’s education, fostering a community-wide commitment to learning.

Funded through crowdfunding campaigns and corporate CSR contributions, this event is a collaborative effort that showcases YAD’s dedication to educational equity. Our university student volunteers played a key role, leading activities and sharing their journeys, which motivated the children to set ambitious goals. By providing tangible resources and emotional support, "School Supplies Donation" empowers young learners to pursue their dreams with confidence.

Student Trip

The "Student Trip" is a flagship event that takes slum community students on an unforgettable journey to explore Cambodia’s cultural and natural treasures. Organized annually, this initiative broadens the horizons of over 200 children, many of whom have never left their neighborhoods. By visiting iconic landmarks like Angkor Wat, the Royal Palace, and Sihanoukville’s beaches, we aim to inspire hope, ignite dreams, and foster a sense of pride in their heritage.

Our latest trip to Siem Reap was a transformative experience. Accompanied by DYTP student mentors, the children explored the ancient temples of Angkor Wat, learning about Cambodia’s history through guided tours. They also visited a local silk farm, where they discovered traditional crafts, and participated in environmental workshops at Tonle Sap Lake, fostering a sense of stewardship for their country’s natural resources.

For 14-year-old Lina, the trip was life-changing. “I never thought I could see such beautiful places,” she said. “Now I want to study history and share these stories with others.” The trip included educational activities, such as journaling and group discussions, which encouraged the children to reflect on their experiences and set personal goals. Evening campfires featured storytelling and traditional music, creating lasting memories and friendships.

Funded through grants and private donations, the "Student Trip" is a testament to YAD’s commitment to holistic development. The involvement of university student mentors ensures a supportive environment, while their leadership skills grow through organizing activities. By exposing children to new possibilities, this event empowers them to envision a brighter future and pursue their ambitions with determination.
